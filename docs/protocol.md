# Protocol Notes

## Working Title

Variation in Microbiology Culture Practices and Culture Results Across CLIF Sites

## Population

Patients represented in CLIF who have at least one microbiology culture collected during the analytic study period.

## Exposure and Stratification Variables

- CLIF site
- Calendar time
- Specimen type
- Encounter or care setting
- Timing relative to hospitalization or ICU course, if available

## Outcomes

- Culture acquisition frequency
- Culture positivity
- Organism and organism-group distributions
- Temporal change in predominant organism groups

## Early Decisions To Lock

- Study window
- CLIF tables and fields used for culture orders, specimens, results, and organisms
- Definition of a unique culture event
- Handling of repeat cultures within patient, encounter, specimen type, and time windows
- Positivity hierarchy for mixed or contaminated results
- Organism grouping rules
